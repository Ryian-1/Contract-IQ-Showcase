# Contract IQ

**Contract Review & Negotiation Workspace | Native macOS Application**

Contract IQ is a LegalTech application designed to bring structure, consistency, and review discipline to contract analysis and negotiation preparation. Developed as a native macOS experience using Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit, with Firebase supporting authentication and application data, Contract IQ brings key clause-level activities into a focused working environment.

The application combines AI-assisted clause drafting and revision with negotiation playbooks, fallback positions, original-versus-proposed clause comparison, revision history, and structured review-package exports. Together, these capabilities support a more organised approach to preparing, assessing, refining, and documenting proposed contractual language.

Contract IQ is built around a clear operating principle: **AI can assist the work of contract review, but legal and commercial decisions remain with the people responsible for them.** The application is intended to strengthen the review process through structured workflows and accessible context—not to replace professional judgment, legal advice, or human approval.

## Product Overview

Contract review and negotiation are iterative business processes. A proposed clause may need to be drafted, compared with the existing provision, assessed against a preferred negotiation position, revised in response to feedback, and retained as part of a review record. When these activities are handled separately, it can become harder to maintain consistency across wording, negotiation positions, and successive revisions.

Contract IQ brings these related activities together in a dedicated desktop workspace. It provides a structured environment for developing clause language, organising preferred and fallback positions, reviewing proposed changes against original wording, maintaining revision context, and preparing review documentation.

The product is intended for workflows associated with legal operations, contract administration, commercial contract review, and negotiation preparation. Its focus is the quality and organisation of the review process: helping users work with contractual language in a more deliberate, consistent, and reviewable manner.

## Core Functional Capabilities

### 1. AI-Assisted Clause Drafting and Revision

Contract IQ incorporates AI-assisted workflows for drafting, refining, and revising contractual clauses. These capabilities support the preparation of alternative language and iterative improvement of proposed provisions.

Rather than treating generated wording as a final decision, the workflow keeps the proposed language within a human-led review process. Users remain responsible for assessing the wording, its commercial implications, and its suitability for the agreement under consideration.

### 2. Negotiation Playbooks and Fallback Positions

Effective negotiation preparation requires clarity around preferred contractual positions and acceptable alternatives. Contract IQ provides a structured way to organise negotiation playbooks, preferred positions, and fallback language.

This capability helps users approach clause discussions with defined alternatives and a more consistent reference point. It also connects clause drafting with the broader negotiation context, rather than treating each wording change as an isolated task.

### 3. Original-versus-Proposed Clause Comparison

Contract IQ supports direct comparison between original contractual wording and proposed clause language. Presenting the two versions together helps reviewers identify and assess the changes being considered.

This comparison-oriented workflow supports closer examination of revised language before it is accepted, further negotiated, or incorporated into a subsequent draft. It is designed to make clause-level review more focused and deliberate.

### 4. Revision History and Human-Led Review

Contractual language commonly develops over multiple rounds of review. Contract IQ includes revision-history and human-review workflows to help maintain context as proposed wording changes.

Keeping revision activity organised supports continuity during iterative drafting and negotiation preparation. AI-assisted suggestions remain subject to human assessment; the application does not make autonomous legal, commercial, or approval decisions.

### 5. Structured Review-Package Export

Contract IQ provides Markdown-based export for structured review material. The resulting output offers a portable format for retaining, sharing, or referring to review information as part of subsequent work.

This capability extends the value of the in-application workflow by making review material available in a documented form beyond the immediate drafting session.

## End-to-End Review Workflow

The product brings together five connected stages of contract-review preparation:

1. **Clause development** — prepare, refine, and revise proposed contractual language using AI-assisted drafting workflows.
2. **Negotiation preparation** — organise preferred positions, negotiation approaches, and fallback wording through playbooks.
3. **Comparative assessment** — examine original provisions alongside proposed language to understand the changes under consideration.
4. **Revision and human review** — maintain context across successive revisions and keep assessment and approval under human control.
5. **Review documentation** — generate structured Markdown review material for retention and further reference.

These stages represent a connected working approach rather than a claim that legal review or contract approval can be fully automated. Contract IQ is focused on improving the organisation and continuity of the work that takes place around clause review and negotiation.

## Product Positioning and Intended Use

Contract IQ is positioned as a focused LegalTech productivity application for clause-level contract review and negotiation preparation. Its capabilities are relevant to work undertaken in areas such as:

- Legal operations and contract administration
- Commercial contract review
- Preparation of negotiation positions and fallback language
- Iterative clause drafting and revision
- Organisation and export of contract-review material

The application concentrates on the review and negotiation-preparation activities described above. It should not be represented as a complete contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform, an autonomous legal decision system, or a substitute for a qualified legal professional.

## Technology and Application Architecture

Contract IQ is developed as a native macOS application. Its technology foundation combines Apple's application frameworks with Firebase services for authentication and application data.

| Application Layer | Technology | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Programming language | Swift | Core application development |
| User interface | SwiftUI | Native macOS interface and presentation |
| macOS integration | AppKit | Platform-level application integration |
| Authentication | Firebase Authentication | Application authentication |
| Data platform | Cloud Firestore | Application data services |
| Review output | Markdown | Structured review-package export |

The native macOS implementation provides a desktop environment tailored to focused contract-review work. SwiftUI and AppKit form the application layer, while Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provide the stated backend foundation.

## Product and Design Principles

### Human Accountability

AI-assisted drafting is intended to support the preparation of contractual language. Interpretation, assessment, negotiation decisions, and approval remain the responsibility of the relevant human reviewers.

### Consistency Across Review Activities

Clause drafting, negotiation positions, comparison, revision, and documentation are treated as connected parts of a broader review process.

### Clarity of Proposed Changes

Original and proposed wording are brought into direct comparison so that reviewers can focus on the language being considered and assess revisions in context.

### Continuity Through Iteration

Revision history and organised review workflows help preserve context as contractual language develops through successive rounds of consideration.

### Structured, Reusable Outputs

Markdown-based review-package export provides a practical format for retaining and referring to review material outside the immediate application workflow.

### Native macOS Experience

The application is developed specifically for macOS, using SwiftUI and AppKit to deliver a purpose-built desktop experience.

## Professional Use and Limitations

Contract IQ is intended to assist with the preparation, organisation, and documentation of contract-review and negotiation workflows. It does not independently determine legal enforceability, interpret an agreement with authoritative legal effect, provide legal opinions, approve contractual terms, or replace advice from a qualified legal professional.

AI-generated or AI-assisted contractual language may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a particular transaction. All proposed wording and review outputs should be evaluated by appropriately qualified professionals against the relevant agreement, governing law, commercial context, and organisational requirements before being relied upon or incorporated into an executed contract.
